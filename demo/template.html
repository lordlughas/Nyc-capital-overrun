How well it works

Tested by rolling-origin backtest: for each test snapshot (Sep 2024, Jan 2025, May 2025) the model was trained only on outcomes already known at that date. Risk shown here is calibrated to observed rates from that backtest; the raw model overstates risk for its highest-scoring projects. Data: NYC Open Data, Capital Projects Dashboard (Citywide Budget and Schedule; Budget Spend History and Variance). Inputs not on this form are treated as unknown, as the model allows. A screening aid for ranking projects, not a cost estimate.
